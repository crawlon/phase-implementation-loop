# Bounded Astra Consultation

The Sol owner retains execution adoption, assignments, scheduling and milestone
acceptance; human decisions remain with the human. A retained planner, when
selected, owns planning content and plan edits under the
[workflow agreement](../../roadmap-planner/references/retained-planning.md).
This bounded consultant supplies advice on a concrete difficult
question; it is not another manager or a standing participant in cohort messages.
The owner commissions advice for delivery/cross-plan decisions; a managed
orchestrator commissions its own technical advice within the assigned outcome.
Each supervises its own job and retains its existing decision/write boundaries.
Implementation and independent-verifier profiles stay unchanged.

## When to consult

Use the [consultation profile](contracts.md#managed-role-profiles) within your role
when an unresolved decision involves:

- Consequential architecture or a shared contract with dependent consumers.
- Substantial uncertainty or competing approaches at a new planning frontier.
- A specific unresolved technical assumption exposed by repeated repairs.
  Roadmap/delivery drift first uses the retained direction-review route when present.
- Conflicting cross-plan evidence or materially ambiguous milestone acceptance.

Classify technical consultation and retained planning using the shared
[request scope check](../../roadmap-planner/references/retained-planning.md#request-scope-and-routing)
before dispatch. Reuse an applicable planner recommendation or recorded technical
decision; do not ask both
actors the same question for comparison. Keep repair logs here, not in routine
planner context. With no retained planner this consultant may advise on an
unresolved strategic question under the existing bounded policy.

For bounded fact gathering, use the shared [research route](../../implementation-orchestrator/references/shared-protocol.md#bounded-research)
before commissioning consequential judgment; research evidence does not replace
that judgment or independent review. First reuse an applicable recorded decision. A settled design, routine next-plan
elaboration, factual update, scheduling choice, or normal outcome checkpoint
does not trigger a call. Do not generate a token-cost saving by weakening the
outcome, acceptance or required assurance. Urgent stops and clear human authority
questions go directly to their proper gate; Astra cannot grant permissions.

## Dispatch and result

Use a bounded read-only subagent with explicit model/effort through supported
controls and minimal context, not a user-owned chat or a fork of the caller
history. If delegation is unavailable, disclose it. Record the trigger and
question in the commissioning role's existing state; do not create a separate
planning bureaucracy.

Give the consultant the question, fixed outcome and relevant acceptance criteria,
constraints and authority boundaries, current plan/contract revision, alternatives
already considered, and decisive evidence or accessible file pointers. Require:

```text
Recommendation and reasoning tied to the evidence:
Proposed plan/contract changes and affected dependencies:
Risks, unresolved assumptions and required human decisions:
Evidence that would change this recommendation:
```

The consultant may inspect the bounded evidence but must not edit canonical
plans/code/state, message orchestrators, dispatch workers, commit, or decide on
behalf of the human. Reuse the same consultant for follow-ups on this question
while its context/evidence remain valid. A distinct question can use a fresh
minimal-context consultant; do not keep it polling or forward routine updates.
Supervise an active consultation to terminal completion; never launch duplicates
or abandon its handle to satisfy an observation limit.

The commissioning role assesses advice against source evidence and records its
disposition. Planning edits go to the retained planner when selected; otherwise
the owner applies them. The owner reconciles execution assignments; the orchestrator
keeps technical changes within its assignment and worker protocol. Preserve human
gates and required assignment acknowledgements. Advice is not execution authority
or a second approval gate.
Unrelated authorized work may continue while the affected decision is unresolved.
If Astra is unavailable or inconclusive, preserve the question and pause only
dependent work; report the limitation and concrete missing evidence or alternative
route requiring approval. Do not silently claim an equivalent consultation or
switch external providers. Routine work remains with its responsible role.

## Independence and cost

If Astra contributed to authoring a design, it cannot provide that design's
required independent review. Use the existing approved independent reviewer
route in a separate context; do not reinterpret consultant agreement as PASS.
Consultation does not automatically add a review when the review policy does
not require one, nor replace a required design/implementation verification.

Reuse the recorded decision until relevant evidence, scope or assumptions change.
Measure consultation count/time, owner usage, rework and accepted outcomes in the
existing pilot records. Keep cached input, new input and output distinct when
available; raw token counts do not establish subscription usage or savings.
