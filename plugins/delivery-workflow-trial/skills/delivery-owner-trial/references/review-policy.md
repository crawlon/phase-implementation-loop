# Review Scope and Timing

Set this policy when preparing an assignment; reuse it at its named checkpoints.
Keep the policy in existing plan/state fields, not another ledger. Cheap tests,
orchestrator inspection, independent phase verification, and owner outcome review
serve different purposes; none substitutes for the others.

## Phase boundaries and normal verification

A phase should deliver a coherent, reviewable behavior or resolve a meaningful
risk. Group small related edits when planning; split work before a risky decision
creates substantial dependent work. Do not split by file or mechanical task just
to show progress. Do not merge frozen phases or skip required reviews mid-run to
save tokens; resolve material plan revisions through existing authority rules.

Keep independent verification before every substantive phase becomes GREEN, and
UI review for affected user-visible behavior. Factual bookkeeping follows the
existing exception. Repair reviews cover the changed boundary and its affected
behavior; reuse valid evidence elsewhere. A new reviewer, handoff, or unrelated
commit does not by itself require a whole-plan audit.

## Design review before consequential implementation

Apply the shared [consequential design review](../../phase-implementation-loop-trial/references/shared-protocol.md#consequential-design-review)
requirements. The owner assigns the independent review and records applicability
and dependent work in the assignment; it cannot independently review its own plan.
Use approved reviewer transport only when needed. Blocking findings gate affected
implementation/consumers; unrelated authorized work may continue.

## Earliest useful integration check

Apply the shared [early integration check](../../phase-implementation-loop-trial/references/shared-protocol.md#early-integration-check)
requirements across the interacting plans. The owner names one integration
orchestrator and an existing integration assignment with the triggering
deliverables, target and dependent work. Missing/failing evidence blocks only
dependent expansion; independent work continues. Route evidence questions to
the owner and missing human authority directly to the executing chat. Keep final
combined milestone verification; do not import standalone outcome-checkpoint timing.

## Outcome boundaries and stagnation

At startup agree a required owner outcome inspection at the next meaningful
capability/dependency boundary, including the evidence expected and actions gated
on it. Select boundaries from delivery and risk. If a long assignment has no nearer
meaningful boundary, use at most three completed substantive phases as a bounded
pilot backstop, not the sole way to notice drift. Short plans may use completion.
Record the selected boundary and notification route in the existing assignment.
Previously agreed phase-count gates remain until explicitly amended.

At a due required boundary, settle owned jobs, save a compact cumulative delta and
notify once. GREEN work keeps its normal commit authority; new dependent phase
starts pause until the owner records actual outcome inspection. Keep WAITING_OWNER,
exact needed decision/inspection, route result and resumer. A receipt, handoff,
planner recommendation or silence is not inspection and does not reset coverage.
If an earlier decision included this actual inspection, record/reuse it rather
than adding another cycle. Gated owner-visible reports may serve the same review.
No new human approval arises merely from an owner coordination gate.

The owner inspects cumulative criterion coverage, usable capabilities, necessary
learning and remaining blockers before advising on the next technical question.
Preparation needs an evidenced blocker/invariant and observable exit. Record the
next result, actor, covered checkpoint sequence and next required boundary. Reuse
valid evidence; inspect deeper for a material gap, contradiction or risk. If
aligned, release only the existing coordination gate and dispatch already approved
work. Drift is resolved under decision classification and assignment acknowledgement;
reserved choices and missing authority stay human-controlled.

Assess stagnation at terminal jobs/checkpoints within unfinished phases, not only
when a phase completes. Expanding preparation, repeated repairs without new evidence,
or repeated postponement of the next result use the shared
[direction-review triggers](../../roadmap-planner-trial/references/retained-planning.md#direction-reviews).
The owner requests the retained planner's cumulative assessment when present,
without giving it worker supervision or canonical writes. Without one, the owner
performs the same direction check and uses bounded consultation for unresolved
consequential questions. Two materially similar red cycles and autopilot's two
distinct failed repairs keep their existing stops; advice never resets a budget.
Continue justified groundwork and required assurance. Optional hardening is a
follow-up only when it is not required for acceptance or safety.

Planner direction reviews are advisory unless a specific required decision is
unresolved; do not add a planner signature to every phase or outcome gate. The
owner remains accountable and dispositions actionable advice before more affected
work. An unavailable advisory planner does not stop otherwise valid execution.
Known dependency/safety risks trigger their actual gates immediately. Unrelated
authorized work can continue while dependent decisions are unresolved.

At existing reviews reuse current local
[context assessments](contracts.md#context-health-and-follow-up); request a local
reassessment for stale/confused evidence without inventing another actor's usage.
Context checks and transfers neither satisfy outcome inspections nor add reviews.

## Tune with evidence

Judge owner effectiveness by correct evidence-based decisions, accepted milestones,
avoidable stalls, duplicated work, rework and integration defects. During the
pilot, use existing receipts to distinguish implementation/verification time,
human waits, owner/planner waits and coordination turns. Review accepted criterion
coverage, repeated repairs, integration defects and actual role usage when exposed. Compare equivalent accepted outcomes;
shorter messages or fewer reads alone do not prove improvement. Adjust future
phase sizes/checkpoints with required authorization. Preserve acceptance and
required assurance; report token savings only when measured.
