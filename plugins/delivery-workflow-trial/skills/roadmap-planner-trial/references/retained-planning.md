# Retained Planning And Trial Agreement

Shared by the trial planner, owner and execution modes. Read at trial selection
or adoption and for substantial planning/direction decisions; routine phases
reuse the recorded agreement. Existing current skills are separate and unchanged.

## Selection and responsibility

Record explicit human trial selection and one configuration in existing delivery
state: owner-managed execution, or standalone orchestration. In either configuration
record `retained planner: [verified binding] | none`. The human can start the
whole workflow once from the existing planner using [workflow startup](workflow-startup.md):
one standalone orchestrator by default, or an owner that launches managed execution
when continuing cross-plan coordination is useful. Reuse approved actors; do not
add another planner. Adding chats, messages, worktrees, trackers or schedules needs
original human authority for those actions and their scope. Selecting the workflow
does not require separate manual selection of every child skill.

The coordinator is the owner for managed execution and the invoking orchestrator
for standalone execution. It owns canonical plan decisions, evidence disposition
and execution routing under its mode. The retained planner owns proposals,
strategic rationale and its own notes. Its bounded startup launch transfers
dispatch to the coordinator after acknowledgement. It may write authorized planner
notes/drafts, but not canonical execution plans, owner indexes, source code or
tracker fields owned by other actors. Standalone substantive plan edits still
go through an edit-capable worker under the shared Role Contract.

The coordinator may adopt a supported proposal only within existing authority.
Fixed outcomes, acceptance, compatibility obligations, hard limits and reserved
human choices remain human-controlled. The planner neither grants execution
authority nor replaces independent verification. A design author cannot provide
its independent review. A strategic recommendation is not a verification waiver.

With a retained planner, use it for substantive roadmap/next-plan proposals and
direction reviews; settled elaboration and routine execution need no call. Keep
bounded technical consultation separate when its specific question justifies it.
Without a planner, the trial owner follows progressive planning with consultation
when needed; standalone uses its existing bounded planning/fallback route.
Do not launch both routes for routine comparison or silently replace an unavailable
retained planner. Pause only a decision that genuinely requires its unresolved
input; continue independent work. Disclose a missing route and the precise recovery
need. A different planner/provider requires the corresponding established authority.

## Agreement and evidence

Use fields in the existing owner index for managed execution, or the existing
phase state for standalone. No additional delivery ledger is required:

```text
Trial version/configuration; original human selection and action authority:
Fixed goal, canonical plan/rationale revision and acceptance pointers:
Coordinator and optional planner: names, IDs/hosts, generations, actual profiles:
Canonical plan/index writer; phase-state writers; planner-note writer/path:
Delegated local decisions, hard limits, resource/repair bounds and pending gates:
Latest criterion coverage; decisive learning/blockers and evidence conditions:
Last outcome/direction review and scope; next observable result/actor:
Required outcome gates; advisory planner triggers and notification route:
Messaging per direction/purpose; successor coverage and actual route results:
Optional heartbeat: approved identity, target binding, scope and lifecycle:
Context assessments, next safe boundaries and switching/handoff pointers:
```

Preserve planning rationale in the canonical plan or linked planner notes:
why this route serves the target, important rejected alternatives, assumptions
and evidence that would change the plan. Keep one current learning summary and
linked history. A retained chat is useful continuity, not the sole durable source.

At reviews, locate evidence through the coordinator snapshot but read the latest
relevant phase checkpoints directly when accessible. Identify plan/checkpoint
revisions and running jobs; do not assume an old summary describes current work.
If sources disagree, resolve the decisive gap before recommending dependent action.
Load logs/diffs only for a named question; technical transcripts are not routine
planner inputs. A fresh retained-planner generation starts from this concise state.

## Direction reviews

The coordinator checks direction locally at named outcome boundaries. Request a
retained-planner review for substantial roadmap changes, cumulative progress that
contradicts the plan, repeated repairs without new evidence, preparation expanding
beyond its agreed boundary, or successive postponement of the next observable
result. Assess these signals at safe job/checkpoint boundaries inside an unfinished
phase as well as at completion. Reuse equivalent valid reviews.

Two preparation-only phases or growing tooling effort trigger the shared local
critical-path check. Two materially similar red repair cycles without new evidence
retain the execution stop. A retained planner examines the disputed approach when
those checks cannot justify continuation; consulting it does not reset a repair
budget or release a gate. A long required verifier job is not drift merely because
it takes time. Its supervisor drives the same handle to terminal completion.

The retained planner compares delivered criteria and necessary learning since
the last review with the original goal and the next milestone. Investigate whether
the original plan is mistaken, not only whether its steps were followed. Distinguish
required groundwork from optional hardening without dropping required quality.
For preparation, name the evidenced blocker/invariant and an observable exit.

Return a compact advisory result, with decisive evidence pointers:

```text
Direction: aligned | revise approach | insufficient evidence
Reviewed goal/plan/checkpoint revisions; evidence limits:
Criteria advanced, necessary learning and remaining delivery blocker:
Current approach and preparation exit: justified basis or specific gap:
Smallest proposed continuation/change; next observable result and actor:
Affected dependencies/gates, human decisions and evidence that would change advice:
```

The coordinator records adoption, rejection with evidence, or the exact unresolved
question and resumer. Evaluate actionable advice before more affected work;
reconcile revisions and invalidate only affected evidence. A delivered message,
planner agreement or “aligned” result is not owner outcome inspection, independent
PASS, commit approval or authority. An advisory request/absent reply adds no gate
to otherwise justified execution. Required review/decision gates remain explicit
and pause only dependent actions; never infer their release from silence.

## Wakeups and economical coordination

Use approved actionable events and named review boundaries by default. Notify the
coordinator once for a supported recommendation or specific decision; do not forward
worker activity or request routine acknowledgements. Resolve current bindings through
the directory and respect message-purpose authority after succession.

An optional separately authorized native heartbeat may initiate a planner review
when neither execution actor requests help. Prefer a modest recovery cadence,
chosen for the actual work, not minute-by-minute management. Read changed/stale
phase evidence and unresolved stalls, even when an owner is active, but skip an
equivalent in-flight/recent review. Unchanged valid progress stays quiet. The planner
reports advice; it does not duplicate owner dispatch or cancel another actor's jobs.
Use native automation controls and preserve approved prompt/model/scope/notification
and lifecycle fields. Record the actual target and disable or rebind only within
authority at completion, cancellation, trial exit or planner succession. If no
scheduler or trusted message route works, disclose manual recovery; no wakeup promise.

## Adoption and switching back

Select or leave the trial at a safe checkpoint after each adopting actor settles
its own jobs. Selection does not authorize new chats, messages, edits or commits.
An existing chat can adopt the new role in place only with reliable context and
explicit user selection; otherwise use an authorized fresh handoff. Do not silently
load both current and trial protocols into the same assignment.

Record the selected skill names/version, preserved workspace/HEAD and dirty ownership,
original action authority, exact pending approvals/reviews/decisions, accepted evidence
and next step. Reconcile role bindings, canonical writer and affected assignments;
acknowledge changed assignments before dependent execution. Existing hard caps,
frozen order and agreed owner-inspection gates remain binding until explicitly
amended through their actual authority. No trial selection retroactively expands them.

Switching back selects the current entrypoints and reconciles their role contract.
The current owner resumes planning ownership; a retained planner's advice does not
automatically grant current-mode messaging or management authority. Pause/rebind
trial-specific automation and routes under their approved lifecycle, preserving
pending questions and exposing unavailable recovery. Leave produced work/evidence
intact; no reset, discard, implicit commit or restarted Phase 1. Handoff preserves
gates and budgets; it does not reset outcome-review counts or create permissions.
